For this new pilot only, an explicit HTTP 503 may be retried at most twice, with 2- and 8-second fallback delays, respecting a `Retry-After` up to 30 seconds. A longer `Retry-After` stops the operation. Every attempt is recorded separately; a timeout or other ambiguous failure is not automatically retried. After exhausted retries or another provider error or missing usage, the current stage stops launching requests. The earlier no-retry registrations and their results are unchanged. The estimated model-call target is below $1 for this small pilot, not a hard cap; failed and unreported charges remain unknown. A successful retry cannot prove the upstream cause of 503 has been fixed.
