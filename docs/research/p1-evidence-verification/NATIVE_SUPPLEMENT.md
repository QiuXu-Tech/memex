## Work that can be delegated honestly

The earlier 22 review templates were a chosen two-reviewer workflow, not a prerequisite for these development runs. Programmatic source checks, independent request-derived regression oracles, trace checks, label drafts, literature checks and manuscript preparation can be completed with explicit provenance. An AI-assisted or programmatic record must never be counted as a human review. Existing human-review-v1 packets and their zero completed count are preserved.

The versioned assisted-review materials describe which observations are deterministic, which are fixture-only, which remain unmeasured, and which require substantive judgment. They do not turn synthetic archives or unaccepted tasks into scientific samples. A human author's actual contribution, affiliation, conflicts and responsibility cannot be invented or supplied by the assistant.

Current TMLR editorial policy permits LLM assistance while retaining author responsibility and excluding LLM authorship. Its FAQ also states an expectation of human-sourced ideas, claims and results; its acceptance criteria require convincing evidence. These pages do not establish a blanket requirement for 22 task-level human review forms, nor do they approve this particular automated oracle design.
