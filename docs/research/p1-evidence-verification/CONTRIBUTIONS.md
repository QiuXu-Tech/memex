# Human contributions, authorship, and AI-use worksheet

**NOT READY — no author list, contribution declaration, or human-origin determination has been verified.** Updated 2026-11-28. This worksheet records missing evidence; it does not attest that anyone has already performed the work below. It is a controller-side preparation document and is excluded from the anonymous review-source ZIP.

## 1. The unresolved condition

TMLR's current editorial policy permits general-purpose LLM assistance, places responsibility on human authors, and excludes LLM authorship. Its FAQ additionally calls for a first-page disclosure and expects the ideas, claims, and results to be human-sourced. The two statements are retained together here; we have not found a primary-source statement explicitly withdrawing that FAQ expectation. A disclosure or signature alone does not resolve the origin of the research. [T1][T2]

The current record documents substantial AI assistance in research formulation, literature/source inspection, drafting, implementation and review, test execution, source acquisition, and packaging. It does **not** establish the identity, work, or independent review of qualifying human authors. Do not replace that uncertainty with “the authors verified everything,” “AI was used only for grammar,” or an invented contribution statement.

Project ownership, authorization to carry out work, selection among proposed options, and approval of a final file are not by themselves a record of substantial intellectual contribution. Record the actual human reasoning and work, including its timing and evidence. Later human analysis may be valuable, but it must be described as later analysis rather than retroactively changing how an idea or result originated.

## 3. Research-origin ledger

This is not a scorecard for awarding authorship. For each item, retain the earliest available records, distinguish human reasoning from tool assistance, and identify what a human subsequently checked. Do not infer provenance from a polished final paragraph.

| Research element | Documented assistance / present state | Missing human-origin or review evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Research question and comparison with existing methods | AI assisted problem framing, overlap inspection, and articulation of the proposed increment. | Dated human conception, critique, or independent reasoning establishing the contribution. |
| Verification and freshness method | AI assisted method formulation, code implementation, regression tests, and boundary analysis. | Human derivation/analysis of the contract, assumptions, counterexamples, and unresolved cases. |
| Task and source selection | Assistants inspected official source records and helped execute source/environment qualification. Candidate acquisition is not accepted P1 task construction. | Human rationale for the target population, sampling, independence, and intended use; independent source checks. |
| Experimental design and statistics | The current manuscript specifies proposed endpoints, comparisons, and analysis choices. Confirmatory method-effect results remain unmeasured. | Human reasoning for the estimands, task denominator, uncertainty analysis, noninferiority margin, and stopping rules. |
| Program execution and engineering observations | Logs record actual commands, output, tests, and component attempts. An executed command is distinguishable from an invented result. | Human interpretation, reproduction, and review of the records; attribution of who designed and validated the experiment. |
| Natural-task labels and adjudication | Independent P1 human annotation is not established in the current draft. | Real annotators, independent labels, disagreements, adjudication, and separation from the method. |
| English manuscript and submission preparation | AI substantially drafted and revised text and prepared the official-template derivative. | Human substantive revision, source verification, and acceptance of the exact final scientific statements. |

For each completed ledger item, add a record using this structure:

```text
Element / section / claim:
Earliest dated record and exact artifact hash:
Initial origin: HUMAN / AI / MIXED / UNKNOWN, with evidence:
Named human's actual intellectual work:
AI tools, version identifiers if known, and specific assistance:
Independent human check: procedure, observations, and limitations:
Corrections or claims removed after that check:
What remains unresolved:
Recorder, reviewer, and dates:
```

Real outputs from software do not by themselves settle the origin of the ideas, claims, or research interpretation. Likewise, a repeated program test is not independent human annotation, and a model's schema-valid response is not evidence that every decision obligation was extracted. Keep these categories separate when filling the ledger.
