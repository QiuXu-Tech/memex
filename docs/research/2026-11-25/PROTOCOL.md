Search and open are separate entry points. Search with openTop exercises both. Do not infer model or real-client latency from this local protocol.

The prospective primary target is at least 30% lower median request latency on the frozen mixed workload. Define the mix by equal contribution from the benchmark's search/open request categories and repeated paired blocks, including misses. Report median baseline and candidate, matched deltas and relative change per fixed case, startup separately, tail latency descriptively, and any negative results. For each other case, report regressions against max(10% of baseline, 0.2 ms). Freeze the request list and repetitions before candidate measurement. One fixture run is a local synthetic study, not a confidence claim about production. Keep raw data and errors.

Safety and quality gates: retain identical full outputs for the same state, including order, ranking, snippets, preview and errors. An accepted runtime change must preserve expiry, updates/deletion, scope and reopen; where the operation is stateful, compare the same sequence of states and not just digests from one snapshot. Use existing relevant regression suites plus a targeted differential for a new semantic risk. Prefer reuse within an operation or only cached SQLite statements over any cross-request result cache. SQLite planning or lexical changes that modify ranking are separate research.

Round 1 maps actual entry points and establishes baseline, isolates the main cost and records a review. Round 2 measures one targeted candidate for the observed cost and gets separate correctness review. Round 3 responds to a concrete limitation or counterexample, repeats paired measurements and evaluates accepted alternatives, preserving negative outcomes. An optimization that preserves parity but misses the target is reported as such. Rejected unsafe changes stay out of the runtime. If static evidence cannot justify a change, measure a safer counterfactual without bypassing correctness to claim a win.

Use local compute and existing Codex quota only. Synthetic store outside user memory. No model call, GPU, release, merge or old UNKNOWN replay. Avoid concurrency between paired benchmark processes. Preserve all snapshots and original reports. Work ends with research branch, PR and readback, plus a scoped cleanup and report.

Frozen before candidate measurement. The warm mixed workload is the exact request sequence in the successful baseline run: 12- and 512-record datasets, 7 cases per dataset, 4 warmups and 12 measured invocations per case per worker, with separate processes in ABBA order. In each of the two paired blocks, pool its 168 measured calls per side (2 dataset sizes × 7 cases × 12). Compare side medians as 1 − candidate/baseline; primary result is the median of the two reductions. For each case compare the paired median absolute delta against max(10% of the corresponding baseline median, 0.2 ms). The A/A run bounds observed variability without claiming a confidence interval. Additional exploratory scenarios and profiling are secondary. Separate process startup does not imply a disk-cold run.
